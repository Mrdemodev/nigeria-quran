Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Qur'an NG ("the App") is developed to help users read and listen to the Qur'an, view prayer information, and locate the Qibla. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information the App Collects

The App itself does not request your name, email address, account credentials, or contacts. Bookmarks, reading progress, quiz progress, downloaded audio, and settings are stored locally on your device.

2. Location Data

The App requests approximate or precise location access to calculate prayer times and determine Qibla direction. Location is processed for those features and may be stored locally with your settings. You may deny or revoke location permission through your device settings. The core Qur'an reading features remain available without location access.

3. Device Services and Storage

The App may use notifications, local storage, network access, media playback, and sharing features. Prayer, daily verse, and daily hadith notifications are scheduled locally on your device. The App does not operate a third-party push-messaging service.

If you share Qur'an text or an image, the shared content is handled by the app or service you choose.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase and Firebase Remote Config, for app initialization, configuration, feature settings, maintenance controls, and force-update settings.
  • Google AdMob, to provide advertisements. Google or its advertising partners may collect or receive advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, and device information for advertising, fraud prevention, measurement, and delivery. Please also review Google's Advertising Privacy Information.
  • Audio-content servers, to stream Qur'anic recitations. These servers may receive the IP address and standard network information associated with your device when audio is requested.
  • Google Fonts, to render text. Font files are bundled with the App.

Third-party providers process data under their own privacy policies. Google's privacy policy is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Internet and Audio Requests

The App requires internet access to stream audio and retrieve Firebase configuration. Standard network information, including IP address, may be transmitted to Firebase or the selected audio server.

6. How Information Is Used

We use information to provide and maintain app features, calculate prayer-related information, retrieve app configuration, display advertisements, prevent abuse or fraud, and improve reliability. We do not sell personal information.

7. Data Retention

Local bookmarks, progress, downloads, and settings remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. Third-party services retain data according to their own policies.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no internet-based storage or communication system can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

10. Your Choices

You can:

  • deny or revoke location and notification permissions in device settings;
  • delete local App data through your device settings;
  • restrict ad personalization in Google's settings where available; and
  • uninstall the App to remove its locally stored data.

11. International Data Processing

Third-party providers may process data on servers outside your country. Their processing is governed by their privacy policies and applicable law.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be reflected by revising the effective date and, where appropriate, in a new App release.

13. Contact

For questions or privacy requests, contact: quranng@gmail.com
